A furnished, 2,000-square-foot loft in Tribeca with a primary bedroom, en suite bathroom, and podcast studio. Open living spaces bring guests together, while an existing recording setup gives conversations and collaborations a place to happen.

Why this works for fomo

The opportunity

A New York home base built around time with creators. The cloutbnb concept pairs a stay in the loft with brand deliverables: creators have a reason to visit, a studio to record in, and a real experience to share.
