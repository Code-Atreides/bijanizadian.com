the quick answers

no guesswork.

Why be the first chapter at my school?

Onboarding is just the start. pump.fun campus is bringing interviews, paid creator briefs and events to campuses through the year, and the first chapter at each school is the one we build those with first.

Who gets the $6.25?

The person who registers and onboards their chapter gets $6.25 for every matched member.

Do I need the whole chapter before I get paid?

No. If your chapter has 150 members, start with 30 and get $187.50. Bring more members later and get paid for them too. Only members who complete both links and match count.

What does a member need to do to count?

Fill out the chapter tracker and sign up on pump.fun through the organizer's referral link. We match the two, then pay the organizer.

Which links go in the group chat?

Both: your chapter tracker link and your personal pump.fun referral link. You get the tracker link when you register.

How do I get paid?

After we match your members, the campus team pays you out. We confirm the payout method with you when you register.

What about professional or business fraternities?

They're in, at half the standard rate: $3.125 per matched member, paid the same way. Social fraternities and sororities get the full $6.25.

Can a club or student organization join?

Yes. Pick "Student organization / club" when you register. The organizer is paid a third of the standard rate, $2.08 per matched member.

Do I have to deposit or buy anything?

No. You don't need to deposit, buy or trade anything to get your onboarding payout, and your members don't either. You can do it in batches.

Does a referral reduce my payout?

No. You keep the full $6.25 per member. If someone referred you, they're paid separately after your chapter onboards: $50 under 60 members, $75 for 60–110, $100 over 110.

Who can take part?

Students who are 18 or older. Every organizer and every member who counts toward a payout must be 18+.

Is this financial advice?

No. pump.fun campus pays for onboarding members, not for trading. Never tell anyone to buy, sell or hold a coin. Crypto is volatile and you can lose what you put in.
