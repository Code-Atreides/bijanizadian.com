How game day works

  • 01Kickoff Both schools' traders are pooled the moment the game starts.
  • 02Every trade counts Each realised gain or loss moves your school's line, live. Open positions don't count until you close them.
  • 03Final whistle Higher PnL takes the day. The scoreboard settles when the game does.
  • 04The season Wins stack up week after week. One school ends the fall on top.

Good to know

Only realised PnL counts
A trade moves the line when it closes, not while it's open.
It settles with the game
Final whistle is final for both scoreboards. No overtime for traders.
Wins carry over
Each game-day win counts toward the season standings.
