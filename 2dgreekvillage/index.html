What can I play?

Three campus games, each part of Arya’s quests. Court Kings at the courts: hit 5 of 10 shots. The Final Drive at fomo field: play quarterback with 1:30 left and score the game-winning touchdown. Flight to New York at the helipad: fly Orangie and Rasmr to the Downtown heliport. High scores save on this device. Game scores are just for fun and never change chapter standings.

How does my house grow?

The connected village displays chapters with more than 30 members. House size grows with your own joined count. Reach 80% of your active roster to unlock a backyard pool, terrace, loungers, and landscaping. All registered chapters remain searchable.

How do I join my chapter?

Ask your chapter lead for your chapter’s invite link. Joining through it counts toward your roster target. Your house and standings update as registrations arrive.

Who gets the $500—and the extra $250?

Every chapter that onboards at least 80% of its active members qualifies for $500, paid to the chapter. The first chapter at each school to reach 80% earns an extra $250. Fraternities and sororities compete for the same first-chapter bonus. Registering alone does not qualify your chapter.

How does Greek Wars work?

Greek Wars is a semester-long trading competition. Your chapter is your team. Chapters compete at their school and nationally on raw profit and loss in dollars. The national trading prize amount will be announced later.

Who can participate?

Recognized Greek chapters, including fraternities and sororities, can enter. Members competing must be 18 or older. The listed Fall 2026 season runs August 24–December 11.
