Onboarding

Three steps, and one number.

The number is 80%. Everything about qualifying, the campus bonus and the national title comes down to how much of your active roster is on fomo, and whether it stays there.

  1. 01Register Your school, your chapter, and your roster size. The roster number is what 80% gets measured against, so give us the real one.
  2. 02Send the link You get one link for your chapter. Drop it in the group chat — every download through it is counted against your roster automatically.
  3. 03Fill to 80% At 80% your chapter is qualified and lands on the map. First frat and first sorority on your campus to get there each take the $250.

What qualifies you

  • 80% or more of your active members onboarded onto fomo.
  • Kept downloaded through the end of the semester. Drop below 80% and the chapter stops counting until it is back.
  • Everyone trading is 18 or over and signed up in their own name.

Actives only — pledges, alumni and everyone on a leave of absence are outside the count, in both directions.

What you get for it

  • A guaranteed $500 for whoever in your chapter finishes the semester up the most.
  • A place on both boards — your school's and the national one — from the day you qualify.
  • fomo perks through the season: trading rewards, events, and first call on everything the campus team runs.
