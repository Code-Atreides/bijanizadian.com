pump.fun brand kit

Parent rules / apply first

pump.fun comes first.

Everything on this sheet comes from pump.fun's portal design reference. It applies to all campus work. The campus sections after it build on these rules and never override them.

Three greens, three jobs. Build on the dark interface and mint controls. Give the brighter green to campaign artwork and campus announcements.

Surfaces
Canvas behind the page, panels to group content, a raised fill for nested controls. All six surface colors come from the live web theme.
Primary action
One mint button per workflow. Keep filters and secondary actions dark.
Mark
Use the original artwork at 24–32 px in navigation. Leave half its width clear on each side.
Type
Geist Sans, Regular 400 and Semibold 600. Tabular numerals for metrics.
Status
Pair each color with a label: blue for review, orange for a requested edit, green for approval.
Campaign art
Give campaign content room to play. Keep submission rows and reporting tables quiet.
Name
pump.fun is always lowercase, with the dot, in copy, headings, labels and alt text.

Identity

The capsule and the campus lockup
PRIMARY LOCKUP / ON CANVAS
pump.fun/campus
pump.fun campusmark + Geist Regular
Sizes
24–32 px in navigation. At least 120 px on a 1080 px social canvas.
Geometry
Use the original artwork as one piece: white cap, dark outline, highlight and the original tilt. Don't recolor, redraw, straighten, outline or add effects.
Lockup
Capsule, then pump.fun in Geist Regular, a mint slash and campus at 72% opacity. On campaign green, set the text in canvas black and the slash in dark green.

03

Campus palette

Built on the web theme

Six dark surfaces, three greens.

Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Dark by default. Campus web pages, the creator portal and reporting graphics live on canvas and panels.

Green by occasion. Campaign green is a field for covers, end cards and announcements, always with the capsule and dark type.

Mint is an action. Interface mint marks the one thing to do next. It isn't decoration or a background.

Campus additions · proposed
Contrast reference +

Body text #EBEBEB on canvas is about 16:1 and metadata #999999 about 7:1, so both are safe for small text. Canvas black on campaign green is about 15:1. Mint text #052E16 on interface mint is about 11:1. The status colors all clear 4.5:1 on panel, but always pair them with a label.

06

Media

Reels + TikTok / campus creators

The clip is the content.

Two layers for your editor. The tag runs the whole video. The end card closes it.

Five rules

  1. Hook in the first second, with a person on screen.
  2. Keep captions inside 72 px sides, 270 px top and 480 px bottom.
  3. Use the app's own text styles for captions. Keep the tag as the only brand layer.
  4. Disclose paid posts with #ad or the platform's paid-partnership label.
  5. Turn every clip in through the creator desk before it's posted.

10

Voice & guardrails

Plain / playful / safe

Writing

Write pump.fun in lowercase. Use short labels that say the next step. One hook and one action per post.

Images

Real students and real campus moments. Campaign content gets room to play: the capsule tile, pump.fun's 3D characters, interview stills. Keep forms and tables quiet.

Facts

Replace placeholders only with confirmed names, dates and counts. Never reuse an old count as current.

Campus guardrails

  • Every creator and campus team member is 18 or older.
  • No financial advice. Never tell anyone to buy, sell or hold a specific coin.
  • No token prices, charts, wallets, balances or gains in campus content.
  • Paid posts carry #ad or the platform's paid-partnership label.
  • Follow each school's rules for events, posters and student groups.
