What we read them for

  • Seats that carry a room. Half your table should be people who bring others — a chapter officer, a captain, whoever runs the group chat.
  • A list you can write today. If you can name all 11 right now, the table is real. If not, it is a plan.
  • A host people say yes to. Not follower count. Whether the things you organise actually fill up.
