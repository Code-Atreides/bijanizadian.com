How the night runs

  1. 01BeforeYou name the room and the people. We book it and cover it.
  2. 02At the tableNo deck, no pitch. Someone from fomo comes as a guest.
  3. 03AfterYou tell us who came and what landed. Good tables get asked back.

That's the entire run of show. There is no hidden second half where somebody stands up and presents something.

What we look for

  1. Seats that carry a roomHalf your table should be people who bring others — a chapter officer, a captain, whoever runs the group chat.
  2. A list you can write todayIf you can name all 11 right now, the table is real. If not, it's a plan.
  3. A host people say yes toNot follower count. Whether the things you organise actually fill up.

What this is not

  • Not a launch party. 12 people, one table, one conversation.
  • Not a focus group. Nobody is going to make your friends rate an app.
  • Not a script. Post what you actually thought of the night — that's what travels.
  • Not a sales dinner. No deck, and nobody from fomo at the head of the table.

Deliverables

What you deliver

  1. 7 days beforeThe list. 11 names, confirmed. We book the table to that number, so it has to be real.
  2. 48 hours beforeAny changes. Drop-outs happen and are fine. A table booked for 12 that seats 6 is not.
  3. On the nightA full table. At least 10 of the 12 seats filled, and everyone 18 or over.
  4. Within 48 hoursThe recap. A few lines: who came, what landed, what didn't. Not a report — an honest paragraph.
  5. To competePost it, and log it. Only if you want the prize — the dinner is covered either way. Post the night however you normally would, then submit the link so the views count toward your campus. Every post has to say fomo paid — that one's a rule, not a preference.

What fomo delivers

  1. On approvalThe booking. We find and reserve the restaurant, or take yours if you already have one.
  2. On approvalSomething to forward. A confirmation you can send your table, so the invite comes from you and looks real.
  3. On the nightThe check, settled directly. Up to $1,200. You never pay, never front it, never file an expense.
  4. AfterYour link, and the credit. Every signup through it is attributed to you, and we tell you the number.

Miss a deliverable and nothing happens to you — we just probably won't book the next one. There's no clawback and nothing to sign.
