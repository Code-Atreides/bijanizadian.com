Charge it first

The two mics charge inside their case, like earbuds.

  1. Leave both mics in the charging case and plug the case into any USB-C charger.
  2. Let it charge fully before your first shoot.
  3. Put the mics back in the case after every shoot, so they're charged for the next one.

Put fomo on the flag

The sticker is one strip that wraps all four sides: fomo, eyes, fomo, eyes.

  1. Slide the flag off the handle and wipe it clean and dry.
  2. Peel the backing and start at a back corner, top edge lined up.
  3. Wrap one side at a time, smoothing each flat so every logo sits centred.
  4. Lay the plain blue end over where you started, then fold the thin top and bottom edges over.

Connect to your phone

The mics come paired to the receiver, so there's no app to set up.

  1. Plug the USB-C receiver into your phone's charging port.
  2. Take a mic out of the case. It turns on and connects by itself.
  3. A solid blue light means you're connected.
  4. Still flashing? Hold the receiver's button for 3 s, then hold the button on the switched-off mic for 6 s. They pair.

Handheld or clip-on

You have two mics, and both record into your phone at once.

HandheldStreet questions and interviews

Fit a mic into the slot on top of the handle, slide the flag on and add the black or grey foam. Hold it a fist below the speaker's chin, fomo side to camera.

Clip-onTalking to camera

Clip the other mic to your shirt, about a hand below your chin. Outside or windy? Put on the furry windshield.

Interviewing someone? Clip one mic on yourself and point the handheld at them.

Film your first post

Open your camera, TikTok or Instagram and record as usual. The receiver is now your phone's mic.

  1. Record a 10-second test and play it back before every shoot.
  2. Shoot vertical, with the fomo side of the flag toward the camera.
  3. Loud spot? Tap the mic once for noise cancelling. Its light turns green.
  4. Keep it about people and campus. Never show anyone's balance or promise returns.
