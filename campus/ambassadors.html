01 / 04The brief

the quick answers

no guesswork.

Is this paid? +

Yes. You earn money for approved results or milestones. For content, we verify each post first, then determine payment based on its performance. Payment details depend on your role and assignment, and the team will go through them with you.

What does a campus intern do? +

You help build fomo’s presence at your school through content, student onboarding, partnerships, or events. Your role gives you a focus. Each assignment sets out what to deliver and how the team will review it.

How do I show my results? +

Follow the proof requirements in your brief: a published post with its insights, an event recap, or verified signups. Submit real results so the team can review the work and determine payment.

Do I need to be in Greek life? +

No. Clubs, sports teams, creative communities, and friend groups all have a place here. Start with the people you know and the communities you understand.

Do I need a big following? +

No. We look for original ideas, good judgment, and follow-through. There are roles in organizing, partnerships, and student outreach as well as content.

What if I don’t know which role to choose? +

Choose “Wherever I’m needed” in your application. Tell us what you enjoy doing and give an example of something you’ve made, organized, or helped grow.

What happens after I apply? +

The fomo campus team reviews your application and may contact you by email or phone to discuss your fit and next steps. Applying does not guarantee a role.
