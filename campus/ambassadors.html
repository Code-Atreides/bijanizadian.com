common questions.

Is this paid? +

Yes. Pay is based on outcomes: the signups, events, posts, and partnerships you deliver. Better results mean better pay, and the team walks you through how it works for your role.

What does a campus ambassador do? +

Every ambassador starts with five short tasks over about two weeks: get set up, sign up a few friends, make a post, map campus orgs, and plan around big campus moments. Then you’re placed in Growth, Content, Culture & Events, or Partnerships.

How do I show my results? +

Each task says what to submit, like a referral count, a post link, or your campus list. The team verifies it and counts it toward your results.

Do I need to be in Greek life? +

No. Greek life can help with connections, but we want students from every part of campus.

Do I need a big following? +

No. We care more about ideas, judgment, and follow-through, and most roles aren’t about content.

What if I don’t know which role I want? +

You don’t have to decide yet. Each starter task is a small version of a role, and your check-in looks at what you did best and enjoyed most. Pick “Not sure yet” on the application.

Can I grow into a bigger role? +

Yes. Ambassadors who deliver can take on more responsibility, including Campus President.

What happens after I apply? +

The fomo campus team reviews your application and may reach out by email or phone. Applying doesn’t guarantee a spot.
