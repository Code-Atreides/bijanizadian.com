the quick answers

no guesswork.

Is this paid? +

Yes. You earn money for approved results or milestones. The targets and payment details depend on your role and assignment, and the team will go through them with you.

What does a campus ambassador do? +

You complete tasks that help fomo grow at your school. Depending on your role, that can mean filming a video, helping students sign up, coordinating an event, reaching out to clubs, or organizing your campus team.

How do I show my results? +

Share what the brief asks for, such as a post link with performance screenshots, an event recap, or a signup update. The team checks your results against the agreed milestone before payment.

Do I need to be in Greek life? +

No. This is for students who want to help build the campus community. Your connections might be through clubs, teams, creators, classes, or the people you already spend time with.

Do I need a big following? +

No. There’s work beyond posting: helping students sign up, coordinating events, reaching out to clubs, and keeping the team organized. Tell us what you’re good at.

What if I don’t know which role to choose? +

Explore the roles above, or choose “Wherever I’m needed” in your application. Use your answer to tell us what you enjoy and where you think you could contribute.

What happens after I apply? +

The campus team reviews your information and can contact you by email or phone about next steps. Applying doesn’t confirm a place on the team.
