Campus team

the people who run it.

Greek Wars does not happen on its own. On every campus a team of five onboards the chapters, brings in the partners, books the dinners, and keeps the scoreboard honest. Read what each seat is, then apply.

  • What it is

    fomo on your campus, run by five students.One team per school, five seats, one campus on the board. You are the reason the chapters know the program exists.

  • 01 · Campus President

    Runs the team, owns the standings.The one fomo calls. Sets the plan for the term, keeps the other four seats moving, and answers for the campus's number.

  • 02 · Growth

    Gets the campus onto fomo.Onboards the chapters, signs up the traders, and turns every game day into more people on the app.

  • 03 · Partnerships

    Brings in the rooms and the orgs.Restaurants, bars, clubs, and the other organisations on campus that host, sponsor, or turn up with their people.

  • 04 · Content

    Posts the nights, and makes sure the posts count.Every post says fomo paid. You check the insights before a view reaches the board.

  • 05 · Culture

    Dinners and events.Books the tables, runs the game-day watch parties, and holds the crew sheet for every night the campus puts on.

What you get

A funded program, the brand, and a line on your resume that means something.

  • Dinners are paid for, up to $1,200 a table, with nothing out of pocket.
  • The brand kit and the merch are yours to use, inside the house rules.
  • $10,000 to the campus that wins the fall.

The commitment

One term at a time, starting Fall 2026. A few hours a week, and heavier on game days and dinner nights. You report to the fomo team and to your own campus standings.

  • Get your chapters on the board before the first live game day.
  • Run every dinner your campus is granted, with a crew sheet for each.
  • Keep it disclosed. Every post says fomo paid. Undisclosed posts are rejected.

The house rules

fomo is always lowercase. It is part of the brand, and it is the first thing we notice in a post.

Only realised PnL counts on game days, and only approved views count for dinners. Claimed numbers never reach the board. Your job is to make sure your campus never has to argue about a number.

Trading involves risk, including loss of principal. Nobody on the campus team gives trading advice, ever.

Who we want: the person people already text when something is happening. Not a title. If your group chats fill up when you say the word, you are the one.
