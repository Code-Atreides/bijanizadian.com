02

Color palette

Shared across formats

Choose the color for the message.

Every colorway below works for grid posts and stories. Keep the palette consistent across a campaign.

One field. One lead accent. Use a solid field or a gradient of neighboring purples. Keep one focal accent. Every treatment can move between a story and a grid post.

Core colors stay consistent. Campus purple is the existing #516AF6 fomo blue. Deep violet and reward cyan, below, are supporting colors for campaign graphics.

Pair for contrast. White or ink on purple for large headlines. Near-black for small text on purple. Ink on cyan and lavender. White on electric and ink.

Supporting colors
Campaign atmosphere / optional

make the
moment big.

Electric → deep violet · one soft glow

Color sets the energy.

Campaign graphics pair a violet field with white display type. Use a gentle gradient and one diffuse glow for milestones and recruitment. Keep the light away from small copy.

Supporting accents. Reward cyan highlights a large amount or key word on violet or ink. Keep white for the rest of the headline and for all small copy.

Keep the logo flat. Atmosphere belongs behind the artwork. Keep the wordmark, eyes, and campus mark crisp and unmodified.

Contrast reference +

White on campus purple is 4.43:1 and ink #0B091F on purple is 4.42:1: reserve both for large type. Use #060510 or black for small copy on purple, or put white copy inside an ink panel. On lavender, keep #516AF6 to large headings and use #4057DF for small accent text.

Ordinary text needs at least 4.5:1; large text needs 3:1. Large means 24 CSS px regular or about 19 CSS px bold. Recheck text over photography and gradients. WCAG reference ↗

Motion / milestone cards

Move the light.
Hold the message.

  1. 01 / Introduce

    Fade in the mark, then bring in the headline and count. About 2 seconds.

  2. 02 / Settle

    Drift the background glow slowly. Keep the type still.

  3. 03 / Hold

    Let the full message sit for at least 3 seconds. Loop every 6–8 seconds; this one runs 7.

Writing

Write fomo in lowercase. Start with what people can do. Use one clear hook and one action. A recruitment layout may add three short steps. Tracked uppercase labels and bold uppercase chapter names can organize announcements.

Facts

Keep amounts attached to their purpose, pay to its context, and fundraisers to the beneficiary. Replace placeholders only with confirmed names, dates, or figures.

Images

Use real campus people and activity. Keep colors natural and text clear. For campaign graphics, use a gentle gradient within one color, diffuse light, and an occasional translucent panel. Keep decorative effects behind the type. Handwriting is a small annotation accent.
