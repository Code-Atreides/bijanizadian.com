fomo brand kit

Parent rules / apply first

fomo comes first.

fomo/campus is an edition of fomo. Everything on this sheet comes from fomo’s own brand kit and applies to all campus work. The campus sections after it build on these rules and never override them.

Both are a soft blue spotlight over a solid field: atmospheric, never shiny or multicoloured. Type on either is white or #EAEDFF.

Pairings
On fomo blue or ink, type and logos are white or #EAEDFF. On pale lavender or white, they are fomo blue or ink.
Backgrounds
Start with one dominant background: fomo blue, fomo ink, pale lavender, or white.
Focal point
Use one strong focal point: product UI, a wordmark, or the headline.
Electric blue
An accent or gradient depth only, never a second competing background.
Avoid
Rainbow gradients, neon green, orange, or yellow, metallic effects, and generic crypto gloss.
Logos
Use the approved white or blue marks with clear contrast. Don’t recolour the eyes or use the full-colour eyes in a lockup.
Type
Aeonik, fomo’s exclusive typeface: Regular 400, Medium 500, Bold 700. Keep copy legible and line spacing open.
Buttons
fomo.family’s pair: a translucent fill behind a 12 px blur, a #CBD0EB hairline at 10%, a 12 px radius, bold 18 px type. Primary #606AF7 at 50%, secondary white at 12%. No glow.
Product & UI
Cross-check the live app. Price data must be real: old data is fine, invented data never is. One feature per frame. Never show user information or private balances.
Name
fomo is always lowercase, in copy, headings, labels, and alt text.

The parent marks, unchanged from fomo’s kit. Campus lockups use this wordmark artwork.

03

Campus palette

Built on the fomo palette

Four backgrounds, straight from fomo.

fomo allows four dominant backgrounds. Each campus colorway is one of them, and each works for grid posts and stories. Keep one colorway across a campaign.

One background. One focal point. Pick one of the four. Electric blue can deepen campus purple as gradient depth, never as a background of its own.

Same colors, campus names. Campus purple is fomo blue #516AF6, and lavender is fomo’s pale lavender. The names change; the colors and rules don’t.

fomo’s pairings. White or #EAEDFF on campus purple and ink. Campus purple or ink on lavender and white. On ink, light blue can highlight one large word or figure.

Campus additionWeb links and solid buttons on light surfaces only. Not for graphics.
Campaign atmosphere

make the
moment big.

fomo brand gradient · electric depth

Color sets the energy.

Campaign graphics start from fomo’s brand gradient: campus purple with a soft light-blue glow in the upper third. Electric blue can deepen the lower corner, as fomo’s kit allows. Keep the light away from small copy.

Emphasis. On campus purple, keep every word white and let scale make the point. On ink, light blue can highlight one large word or figure.

Keep the logo flat. Atmosphere belongs behind the artwork. Keep the wordmark, eyes, and campus mark crisp and unmodified.

Contrast reference +

fomo’s pairing puts white or #EAEDFF on campus purple. White there is 4.43:1, just under the 4.5:1 target for small text, so keep small social copy at 32 px or more on a 1080 px canvas (36–48 px is better). On the web, set long small copy on ink or white rather than on purple. On lavender and white, use ink for small text and keep campus purple for large type.

Ordinary text needs at least 4.5:1; large text needs 3:1. Large means 24 CSS px regular or about 19 CSS px bold. Recheck text over photography and gradients. WCAG reference ↗

Motion / milestone cards

Move the light.
Hold the message.

  1. 01 / Introduce

    Fade in the mark, then bring in the headline and count. About 2 seconds.

  2. 02 / Settle

    Drift the background glow slowly. Keep the type still.

  3. 03 / Hold

    Let the full message sit for at least 3 seconds. Loop every 6–8 seconds; this one runs 7.

Always export the complete still frame too, for reduced motion and anywhere that doesn’t autoplay. Fade the mark in, but never spin, morph, or blur it.

Let scale do the work. One oversized headline or number creates the focal point. Counts can reach 180–320 px on a 1080 px canvas. Keep supporting copy smaller and comfortably spaced.

Use two distinct voices. Tight bold display type for the hook; small, widely tracked uppercase for an eyebrow or school name. Never stretch the letters to fill a line.

Fonts & existing templates +

Use licensed Aeonik in your design tool. Editable SVGs reference Aeonik with a sans-serif fallback; PNGs preserve the intended type. Font files are not included in the downloads.

Web body copy runs at Medium 500, as on fomo.family; Regular reads too light at text sizes. The earlier fundraiser story used Poppins and a black and cyan palette. New work uses Aeonik and the four fomo backgrounds. Use logo artwork for the wordmark.

Writing

Write fomo in lowercase. Start with what people can do. Use one clear hook and one action. A recruitment layout may add three short steps. Tracked uppercase labels and bold uppercase chapter names can organize announcements.

Facts

Keep amounts attached to their purpose, pay to its context, and fundraisers to the beneficiary. Replace placeholders only with confirmed names, dates, or figures.

Images

Use real campus people and activity. Keep colors natural and text clear. For campaign graphics, use fomo’s gradients, one soft glow over a solid field, and an occasional translucent panel. Keep effects behind the type: no rainbow gradients, neon, metallic effects, or crypto gloss. Handwriting is a small annotation accent.
