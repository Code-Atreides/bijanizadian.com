02 / color with a job

Two blues.
Different roles.

fomo blue carries the identity. Cyan is the signal on dark social graphics. Choose one lead accent per composition.

Let the background do most of the work.

As a starting composition: roughly 80% base, 15% type and imagery, 5% accent. A full blue title card is the exception: blue becomes the base. Partner logos keep their own approved colors.

04 / the campus mark

Same eyes.
A campus point of view.

The supplied graduation-cap SVG is the master for new campus work. Keep the eyes, cap, and tassel exactly as drawn.

01

Give it space

Leave at least ¼ of the full mark’s height on every side. Measure from the outermost cap and tassel, not just the eyes.

02

Keep it whole

No stretching, tilting, outlines, shadows, gradients, or separate cap colors. Start at 40 px wide on web and 120 px on a 1080 px social canvas; inspect the tassel at final size.

03

Keep identity clear

Use the cap for campus identity. Keep the original eyes for the parent fomo brand and existing approved campaigns. Use actual wordmark artwork when a wordmark is needed.

The white SVG retains the supplied master. Blue and ink downloads change only the fill. These color variants, minimum sizes, and clear-space rules are proposed campus standards. School and chapter marks remain separate partner identities.

Use the family we already use.

Aeonik Medium for web headlines. Regular for body and supporting text. Bold for social headlines, emphasis, and compact labels.

Web
Headlines 48–72 px desktop, 36–48 px mobile. Body 16–18 px with 1.5–1.6 line height.
Social at 1080 px
Headlines 96–160 px. Details 36–48 px. Keep essential text at least 32 px, then check at phone size.
Spacing
Headlines about 1.0–1.1 line height, lightly tightened tracking. Body text gets room; avoid compressed paragraphs.

The existing fundraiser template uses Poppins ExtraBold for display and Aeonik for details. Preserve it when matching that approved template. Use Aeonik for new templates. The logo and wordmark are artwork, not typed substitutes. Use your licensed font files; webfonts are not included as downloads.

Voice / confident, direct, local

Sound like someone
on campus.

Write “fomo” in lowercase. Lead with the thing people can do. Keep headlines short, with one useful detail and one action. Lowercase is our default; big uppercase chapter names belong on announcement graphics.

Be specific. Say “create content,” “bring your people,” or “send your work.” Avoid vague hype, corporate recruiting language, and forced slang.

Keep facts attached. Amounts need a purpose. Pay needs context. Fundraisers need a beneficiary. Use editable placeholders until names, figures, or dates are confirmed.

Let the moment lead. Use real campus people and real activity when available. Keep photos natural and logos clear. Reserve planets and glows for campaign art. A short handwritten CTA can be an accent, not the main type system.
