    Turn in content
    What is fomo campus media?

    fomo's campus content push. Students film what's actually happening at their school and turn in their videos. The best ones live here on the wall and on our Instagram, @fomo.campus.

    What should I film?

    Real moments on your campus: street questions, game days, dinners, chapter events, a day in your life. Shoot vertical. Keep it about people and campus, and never show anyone's balance or promise returns.

    What are media bounties?

    Paid briefs. Film what a bounty asks, turn it in with that bounty ticked, and the fomo team picks the best video for the prize. The current one is Bar videos: the best fomo video at a bar on your campus, app in hand (21+). See the bounties.

    What gear do I need?

    Your phone is enough. If you were sent the content kit, set up your mic first: it takes five minutes and makes every take sound better.

    How do I turn it in?

    Paste a link in Turn in: the TikTok or Instagram you posted, or a Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer file with sharing set to anyone with the link. After your first video it remembers you, so the next one is just the link.

    Do I need to edit it first?

    Yes. Send it finished and ready to post: vertical, trimmed, with any captions or music already on it.

    Can I add music?

    Yes. Add it before you turn the video in, the way you'd post it.

    Do I get credit?

    Add your handle when you turn something in, and it shows on your video here.
