01 · Your canvas

make things happen. four ways to make it yours.

Nobody hands you a campaign calendar. These are the four levers you have, and how you pull them is the job.

  1. 01PeopleBring the right people in. The ones whose group chat is the campus.
  2. 02ChaptersGet organizations competing. A chapter is a clan, and clans want a scoreboard.
  3. 03MomentsCreate things people talk about. A dinner, a watch party, a rivalry.
  4. 04ContentYour campus, your people, your story — in your voice, not a script.

02 · What fomo is

know what you're putting your name on.

You will be asked what this actually is, probably in a group chat, probably at midnight. This is the answer.

A trading app for the rest of us

Sign in with an Apple ID and trade Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana. No gas fees, no bridging, nothing to set up before you can do anything.

The part that makes it different: positions are social. You can see what the people you follow are actually holding, tied to who they are. Everywhere else that lives in a private spreadsheet.

That visibility is the product and the pitch. It is also why the rules further down about other people's positions exist.

03 · The national picture

your campus. your leaderboard.

Every campus running fomo lands on a national board, and the board is the point: it is the thing your people check. Alabama, Texas, Miami, USC, San Diego State — the names change every week.

Any rankings shown in materials are invented. No real participation, affiliation, trading performance or financial returns are represented by them. The live board is at greekwars.

04 · Greek Wars

your chapter is on the leaderboard.

A semester-long competition between chapters. $500 — a promotional competition prize — to the top trader in every qualifying chapter, and the national title on top of that.

  1. 01QualifyMeet the entry criteria — 80% of the chapter's actives onboarded, and kept there.
  2. 02CompeteThe whole semester. Ranked on raw PnL, not points, not volume, not activity.
  3. 03RecogniseEvery qualifying chapter crowns a winner. One chapter takes the country.

The full rules, the map of who is onboarded and the live board are on the Greek Wars page. Chapters register at onboard.

07 · Your first move

you make it move. start small.

Nobody's first week is a launch event. The order matters more than the size — a full table of people you actually know beats a flyer in every building on campus.

  1. 01
    Find your peopleStart with communities you are already in. You do not need permission to talk to your own group chat.
  2. 02
    Bring them on fomoGet your circle on the app through your link, so it counts toward the tasks above.
  3. 03
    Launch your campusGive people a reason to gather — a dinner, a watch party, the first chapter to register.
  4. 04
    Make some noiseTell the story in your campus's voice. Yours, not a script we sent you.
  5. 05
    Climb the leaderboardBuild participation and keep it there. The board rewards the campus that stays, not the one that spikes.

09 · Not up to you

most of this is your judgement. this part is not.

Four rules. They are not style preferences — three of them are the law, and the fourth is what happens if you ignore the first three.

  1. Rule 01

    Say when fomo paid

    Nothing here is conditional on posting — not a dinner, not a prize. But when you do post about something fomo paid for, say so in the post.

    • #ad, paid by fomo or fomo covered this all do the job.
    • A comment underneath does not, and neither does a caption added later.
    • An undisclosed post is rejected — it does not count toward a task or a prize.

  2. Rule 02

    Describe the app, never the outcome

    This is a trading app, and the line matters. Describe what it does. Never what it will pay.

    • No promised or implied returns, and nothing that reads as easy money.
    • A prize is a prize. It is not a trading return, and it must not be shown as one.
    • Never post someone else's positions without asking them first.
    • Never pitch or sign up anyone under 18.

  3. Rule 03

    Use the name properly

    Two things, and the first one catches everybody out.

    • fomo is always lowercase. Never Fomo, never FOMO — not at the start of a sentence either.
    • Do not make official-looking material: no accounts that look like fomo's, no speaking as if you decide policy.

  4. Rule 04 · Ends it

    The short list

    None of this is about performance. A quiet campus is not a problem; these are:

    • Undisclosed paid posts, after a warning.
    • Presenting trading as guaranteed income.
    • Fabricated numbers — bought engagement, invented signups, edited metrics.
    • Signing up anyone under 18.
