A NOTE ON THIS SPACE

Keep the useful things.

This is agencykit, our shared GTM library. A place to organize the original pages, portals, forms, and tools we can build on again.

Search the archive or browse Skeletons to choose original work. Every piece opens with the original first. Choose “Skeletonify” inside to preview its neutral version and download an editable HTML page. The skeleton has neutral content and local demo interactions; client services and real data are connected separately.

Search by project, page type, visual style, or feature. Combine choices with “or”, leave something out with “except”, and we’ll flag spelling matches. Search stays within this catalog. Project profiles bring original work, client branding, editable drafts, and saved versions together. Choose “Use in project” to start an independent draft. Sign in to sync your project workspace. Saved bookmarks and collections stay in this browser. The assistant is a local catalog guide for now; its AI connection is still to come.

For us, and whatever comes next.
